How we build it

Five stages, from CC-News to the platform

01

Data

CC-News (stanford-oval/ccnews): 4–6 outlets, deduped, topic-labeled

02

Training

LoRA on a 1B–4B model, conditions as control codes, MLX-LM on Mac

03

Grounding

Retrieval over the corpus for facts; Scrapling fills coverage gaps

04

Evaluation

Outlet classifier, stylometrics, bias against external ratings, claim check

05

Platform

Explorer plus generator that marks patterns in the text

Five variants compared: prompt only, few-shot, one LoRA, LoRA per outlet, LoRA with retrieval

Advanced AI · IE University · Project I

Walk the five stages left to right. Data comes from CC-News, already extracted and dated, so we do not depend on scraping. Training is LoRA on a small model with outlet, topic and date written into the prompt. Retrieval supplies the facts, because LoRA teaches style, not knowledge. Evaluation has four checks: an outlet classifier, stylometric features, bias against external ratings, and a claim check. The comparison of five variants is the core result of the report.
